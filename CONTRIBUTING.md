# Contributing

Vibe Notepad is a personal project, shared as is. You're welcome to clone it, fork it and build your own
version under the [MIT license](LICENSE).

## Issues are welcome

Found a bug or have an idea? Open an issue.

- **Bugs**: what you did, what happened, what you expected, and your macOS version. If the problem is with a
  particular file, a small sample that shows it helps a lot.
- **Ideas**: what you'd like to do and why. Every issue gets read, but the roadmap follows what the app needs day
  to day, so there's no promise an idea will be built.

## Pull requests aren't accepted

This repository doesn't take pull requests, and they're turned off in its settings. If you've built something
worth sharing, describe it in an issue, or keep it in your fork.
